About

Behind the camera, still figuring it out — one frame at a time.

I'm Mayzal — most people call me Ijal, though a few sources know me as thszal.

My interest in photography started back in middle school, and it never really left — what began as messing around with a borrowed camera turned into something I now take seriously. In 2026 I graduated, and around the same time I started taking on freelance photography work in parallel.

Right now I work steadily with one vendor, shooting a mix of portraits, events, and whatever else the moment calls for — all through my own camera. Every project starts the same way: pay attention, wait for the frame to arrive, and press the shutter before it's gone.

This site is where I keep the shots I'm proud of. The gallery is a work in progress, same as I am.

I'd rather shoot under than over.

Given the choice between a frame that's slightly too dark or slightly blown out, I'll take the dark one every time — you can recover shadow detail, you can't recover a blown highlight. It's less a technical rule and more how I think about restraint in general: hold back a little, and there's always something left to work with.

How a shoot happens

01

Scout & plan

Understand the brief, walk the location if I can, and work out where the light will actually be.

02

Shoot

Stay light on gear, stay patient with timing, and lean under-exposed rather than risk blowing highlights.

03

Edit & deliver

Cull hard, edit lightly, and hand off a set that still looks like the moment it was shot in.
